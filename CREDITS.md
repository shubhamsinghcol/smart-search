# Credits

Medical vocabulary and associations are adapted from these resources. Dataset and dependency terms are separate from the add-on author's code.

- **Mondo Disease Ontology**, Monarch Initiative, [project](https://mondo.monarchinitiative.org/), CC BY 4.0. The pack imports nonobsolete labels, exact synonyms, and direct subclass relationships.
- **Human Phenotype Ontology (HPO)** and disease–phenotype annotations. Smart:Search uses HPO 2026-09-01 and annotations 2026-09-02. [Project](https://hpo.jax.org/) · [License and use conditions](https://hpo.jax.org/license). Citation: Gargano MA et al. *The Human Phenotype Ontology in 2024: phenotypes around the world.* Nucleic Acids Research 52(D1), D1333–D1346. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkad1005).
- **NLM RxNorm Current Prescribable Content**, September 8, 2026. [Resource and documentation](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/research/umls/rxnorm/docs/prescribe.html). Used for drug terminology.
- **BGE-small-en-v1.5**, [BAAI embedding model](https://huggingface.co/BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5), MIT license. Model copyright and license are included with the package.
- **FastEmbed, ONNX Runtime, NumPy, Tokenizers**, and other packaged dependencies: upstream license notices are included in their bundled distributions.

The current terminology pack does not include MeSH, First Aid text, or OptimusKG composite data.
